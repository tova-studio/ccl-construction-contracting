About Us

Who are CCL Construction Contracting?

CCL Construction Contracting has established a reputation as an innovative and effective supplier of high quality civil engineering products and contracting services delivered on time, on budget and to customer standards. This is based around a proven knowledge of the industry, combined with dedicated staff and a strong and highly dedicated management team.

Employing over 80 staff in areas of project management, sub-division development, civil engineering, heavy earthworks, pavement construction and road maintenance, drainage, carpark development and residential and commercial excavations. Our extensive experience in all civil construction areas allows us to offer clients a full range of services.

CCL maintains a modern fleet of over 50 machines, with one of the most advanced 3D machine control fleets locally available. With our in-house engineers and surveyors we deliver fast and efficient solutions to all clients.

Questions customers ask us

Where is CCL Construction Contracting based?

We're in Christchurch. Our addresses are 279 Montreal St, Christchurch, and our office at 363 Hasketts Road, Yaldhurst, Christchurch.

What types of projects do you take on?

Earthworks, civil engineering, commercial siteworks, road construction and maintenance, residential foundations, retaining walls, heavy haulage, and surveying and testing. We also do sub-division development, drainage and carpark development.

Do you have your own engineers and surveyors?

Yes. Our engineers and surveyors are in-house, which helps us deliver fast and efficient solutions to all clients.
